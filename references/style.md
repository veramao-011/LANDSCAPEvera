# Visual specification

## Palette

Color values are approximate targets, not guaranteed output pixels. Keep white visually dominant.

| Role | Color | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#FFFFFF` | White canvas and open surfaces |
| Main line | `#5656D9` | Silhouettes and important edges |
| Secondary line | `#8C91DF` | Background and fine details |
| Lavender | `#E4E0FA` | Restrained, clearly bounded shading |
| Cyan | `#A7DDED` | Subtle water or glazing accents |
| Pale water | `#EAF7FC` | Water plane, sparse reflections |

## Drawing

Use precise architectural drafting, crisp edges and a clear foreground/background line hierarchy. Maintain legibility at print size. Use flat or subtly shaded planes; avoid heavy shadows, fog, paper grain, watercolor bleed or photorealistic rendering. Show spatial transitions through readable paths, ramps, terraces and boundaries.

Draw skies using fine, light, evenly spaced horizontal hatching. Suggest water with a few horizontal or gently curving lines. Use selective paving joints with generous blank areas. Vegetation should be sparse, with slender open branches and sharp, irregular leaf clusters; use only a few separate reed or grass strokes. Avoid dense repeated marks and circular cartoon crowns.

Review the result for unwanted people or text, site geometry drift, overly pale contours, excessive vegetation, moire and uncontrolled textures. Allow explicitly requested overrides.
